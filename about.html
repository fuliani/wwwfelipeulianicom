About

20 Years. Six Companies. One Focus.

Building the infrastructure that helps organizations pay people fairly, design careers intentionally, and compete for talent strategically.

The Short Version

I am a compensation and HR strategy practitioner with over two decades of experience building pay programs, job architectures, and total rewards frameworks at some of the world’s most complex organizations.

I have sat across the table from CEOs arguing why a salary structure matters. I have rebuilt global job frameworks from scratch. I have led pay equity remediation efforts that touched thousands of employees. I have watched well-intentioned compensation programs fail because the architecture underneath them was broken.

This site is where I document what I have learned — not to sell anything, but because these ideas are worth writing down clearly.

Career History

My work spans Fortune 50 corporations, mid-market global businesses, and high-growth organizations. The common thread: building compensation and HR infrastructure that actually works.

At Microsoft, I operated within one of the most sophisticated compensation ecosystems in the world, developing deep expertise in market pricing, pay equity methodology, and large-scale job architecture.

At Tyco International, I led total rewards work across a complex global organization navigating significant structural change, building frameworks that had to work across geographies and business units with very different cultures.

At Herc Rentals, I built and scaled compensation infrastructure for a workforce spanning field operations, corporate, and executive populations — a span that demands simplicity and clarity in how you design and communicate pay.

At AutoZone, I worked within a high-volume, operationally rigorous culture where compensation decisions are made at scale daily. Discipline, consistency, and manager-friendly tools are non-negotiable.

At Spirax Sarco, I led global total rewards for a FTSE 100 industrial technology company, navigating multi-country pay equity requirements, global benefit harmonization, and executive compensation governance across a complex international structure.

Most recently, I have been building the compensation and total rewards function at a Nasdaq-listed enterprise software company, leading job architecture redesign and driving AI adoption in compensation processes.

What I Write About

My writing focuses on the decisions that HR leaders and CHROs face when building or rebuilding compensation infrastructure:

  • Compensation strategy — philosophy, structure, and the decisions behind how organizations position pay
  • Job architecture — leveling, career frameworks, and the structural work that makes everything else possible
  • Total rewards — how to design programs that actually move retention and engagement
  • Pay equity — analysis, remediation, and communication with integrity
  • AI in HR — where automation genuinely helps and where it creates new risks
  • HR leadership — how to build a people function that earns influence with the business

Build to Multiply

Separately, I founded Build to Multiply, an advisory firm offering compensation consulting services to organizations and wealth strategy frameworks for professionals. This site — FelipeUliani.com — is my personal knowledge platform, not a commercial offer. The ideas here stand on their own.

A Note on Perspective

Everything I write reflects my own views and professional experience. It does not represent the positions, policies, or opinions of any current or past employer. I write from what I have seen work and what I have watched fail — across many organizations, contexts, and economic cycles.
