Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how felipeuliani.com (“I,” “me”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this site.

Information I Collect

  • Information you provide. When you submit the contact form or subscribe to the newsletter, I collect the information you enter — such as your name and email address.
  • Usage data. I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser. This data is not used to identify you personally.

How I Use Information

  • To respond to messages you send via the contact form
  • To send new article notifications if you subscribe
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve content

I do not sell your information. I do not share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Providers

I use a small number of service providers, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions
  • MailerLite — manages newsletter subscriptions and delivery
  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics for usage measurement. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings; the site will continue to function normally.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained as long as needed to respond to your message. Newsletter subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe — every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information I hold about you by emailing hello@felipeuliani.com.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some book links on the Resources page are Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
